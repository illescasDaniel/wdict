Part of the "eight" fix (v0.8.0): the English voices' trained clusters were taken to be only aɪ aʊ eɪ oʊ ɔɪ, so `əʊ` and
`ɐʊ` were treated as untrained split pairs and rewritten to `oʊ` (it also protected oak `/əʊk/` from the word-initial ə -> ɐ
rule). Disproved the next day: [piper-gb-diphthong-kept-split](piper-gb-diphthong-kept-split.md).
